# 🎮 Bloxd.io Marketplace

A free community platform for sharing Bloxd.io schematics and texture packs!

## 🌐 Live Website
Visit: **https://Rustyweasel121212.github.io**

## ✨ Features

- 📤 **Free Upload** - Share your schematics and texture packs
- 🔍 **Browse & Search** - Find amazing creations from the community
- 📥 **Download** - Get files with one click
- 🎨 **Modern Design** - Beautiful dark gaming theme
- 📱 **Responsive** - Works on all devices
- 💾 **Persistent Storage** - Your uploads are saved

## 📁 What You Can Upload

- **Schematics** - `.bloxdschematic` files
- **Texture Packs** - `.zip`, `.rar`, image files

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Visit the website
2. Click **Browse** to see existing uploads
3. Click **Upload** to share your creation
4. Fill in the details and submit
5. Your file appears in the marketplace!

## 🛠️ How It Works

- **No Account Needed** - Just upload and share
- **Local Storage** - Files saved in your browser
- **Instant Upload** - See your creation immediately
- **Free Forever** - No fees, no limits
